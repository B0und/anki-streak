# Anki Streak

Anki Streak shows a learner’s AnkiDroid review activity on their Android home screen.

## Language

**Anki study day**  
A day as counted by AnkiDroid. The current study day determines whether a review counts as today’s activity.

**Review mood**  
The character’s expression for the current Anki study day. The character waits until at least one card is answered, then cheers.

**Mood preview**  
A temporary expression selected in the dashboard. The home-screen widget follows the review mood.

**Widget publication**  
The content shown on every Anki Streak widget. A publication shows either setup guidance or review stats with a themed portrait.
